Privacy Policy

This policy explains how BARO (“the developer”) handles information in the iPhone app “Protein Log - SIAPP” (Japanese name: タンパク質, “the app”).

Original policy effective: October 12, 2026 · English version added: October 25, 2026

1. Information used by the app

The app stores information you enter, including food names, protein amounts, meal dates and times, daily goals, favorites, custom foods, and settings, on your device to provide its features.

The app has no feature that sends or collects these records on a developer-operated server. No registration or login is required.

2. Storage and sharing

Records are stored primarily in SwiftData on your device. The minimum information needed for the widget, such as the date, today's total, and daily goal, is shared between the app and its widget through an App Group on the same device.

The app does not provide its own cloud synchronization service. The developer has no system for remotely viewing your records.

3. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The app shows no advertising and includes no advertising SDK or developer-added analytics SDK. It does not track users across apps or websites.

4. In-app purchases

Pro purchases and restoration use Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes purchases and payment information through its services. The app checks purchase status, but does not send payment information or food records to a developer-operated server.

For Apple's handling of information, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Retention and deletion

Local records generally remain until you delete them in the app or delete the app. You can edit or delete individual records. Deleting the app normally also removes its local app data.

Device backups and purchase history held by Apple are managed through Apple's services and your settings. There is no developer-operated user account, so there is no separate account deletion process.

6. Support inquiries

If you contact the developer by email, your email address and inquiry are used as needed to reply and investigate the issue. This information is not automatically collected by the app and is not used for purposes other than handling your inquiry.

Contact: siapp.support@gmail.com

7. This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no developer-added advertising, analytics, or tracking features. For information handled by the hosting provider, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

8. Changes

If the app's features or data handling change, this page will be updated and an effective or revision date will be shown where appropriate.

For questions, visit the support page.
