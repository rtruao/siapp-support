Calories Privacy Policy

This policy describes how SiApp handles information in the Calories iPhone app. Effective November 14, 2026.

On-device records

Your calorie entries, food names or notes, dates and times, daily goal, favorites, custom foods, and settings are stored on your device using SwiftData and local storage. The app requires no account and does not sync these records to a SiApp server.

Widget

The app and widget share summary information, including your current daily total and goal, through an App Group on the same device.

Purchases

Apple StoreKit handles Pro purchases and restoration. The app verifies purchase status. Apple processes payments, and SiApp does not receive your payment details.

Network access and external links

The app has no ads, analytics SDKs, or tracking. Opening support or privacy links in Settings connects your browser to GitHub Pages. For information about hosting-related traffic data, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Deletion and contact

You can delete records in the app. Deleting the app normally removes its local app data. iOS backups are governed by Apple's services and your device settings. If you email us, we use your address and message to respond and investigate the issue. Contact siapp.support@gmail.com.
