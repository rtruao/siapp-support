SIAPP / CalorieTracker

Calories Support

Calories is an iPhone app by SiApp for logging food calories and checking your daily total against a goal you set.

Contact

For help with the app or a purchase, email siapp.support@gmail.com. Include the app version, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue.

Calculate calories from weight

Save a custom food with a reference weight (g) and energy (kcal), then enter the weight you ate. You can change your daily goal in Settings.

Manage records

Food records are stored on your device. You can edit or delete individual records in the app.

Restore Pro

Use the same Apple Account you used for the purchase, then choose Restore Purchases in Settings.
