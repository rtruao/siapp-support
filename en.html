Protein Log - SIAPP Support

Help and frequently asked questions for the iPhone app “Protein Log - SIAPP” (Japanese name: タンパク質).

Contact

For questions about the app, problems, or purchases, email:

siapp.support@gmail.com

Please describe the issue and include your device model and iOS version. Do not send passwords or other confidential information.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my records stored?

Food names, protein amounts, and history are stored on your device. There is no account registration or synchronization with a BARO server.

Can I use the app for free?

Yes. Basic protein logging is free. You can save up to 5 favorites and 1 custom food. The free version includes the current calendar month's history, the current week's graph, and the widget.

What does Pro include?

Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase, not a subscription. It removes the limits on favorites and custom foods, and unlocks past history and monthly and yearly graphs. Check the purchase screen in the app for the current price in your storefront.

My Pro purchase is not showing.

Make sure you are using the Apple Account used for the purchase, then use Restore Purchases in Settings or on the Pro screen. If the issue continues, contact us using the email above.

How do I delete records?

You can edit or delete individual records in the app. Deleting the app normally also removes its local app data. Device backups and Apple purchase history are managed through Apple's services and your settings.
